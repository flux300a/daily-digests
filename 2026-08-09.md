# Daily Digest — 2026-08-09

## HIGHLIGHTS — Notable releases & high-signal repos for systems builders

1. **ClickBench Playground** — Interactive benchmark playground for 110+ database systems (ClickHouse). Lets you run and compare OLAP workloads across engines in-browser. Why it matters: fastest way to evaluate DB choices for analytical workloads without spinning up infrastructure. [HN Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49186732) | [Playground](https://benchmark.clickhouse.com/playground/)

2. **vLLM Deep Dive** — "Inside vLLM: Anatomy of a High-Throughput LLM Inference System" (2025, but resurfaced). Comprehensive breakdown of PagedAttention, continuous batching, and KV cache management. Why it matters: reference architecture for anyone building or optimizing LLM serving stacks. [HN Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49202852) | [Article](https://www.aleksagordic.com/blog/vllm)

3. **Oxide RFD 284: Loading the Host Operating System** — Request for Discussion on host OS boot/load process in Oxide's rack-scale architecture. Why it matters: rare public systems-level design doc from a team building custom firmware-to-OS handoff. [HN Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49175246) | [RFD](https://rfd.shared.oxide.computer/rfd/0284)

4. **Finding Zombies in Our Systems** — Pinterest Engineering case study on CPU bottlenecks from "zombie" processes (orphaned goroutines, leaked threads, stuck GC). Why it matters: practical observability patterns for diagnosing silent resource leaks in production Go/Java services. [HN Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49145193) | [Article](https://medium.com/pinterest-engineering/finding-zombies-in-our-systems-a-real-world-story-of-cpu-bottlenecks-ea4722e552eb)

5. **Water System Controllers on the Internet** — Ex-NSA chief warns against exposing industrial control systems after suspected Iran-linked attacks. Why it matters: timely reminder that OT/IT convergence without air-gapping is a systemic risk; relevant for anyone building infra that touches physical systems. [HN Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49216362) | [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/08/07/water-system-controllers-dont-belong-on-the-internet-says-ex-nsa-chief-after-suspected-iran-attacks/5285070)
